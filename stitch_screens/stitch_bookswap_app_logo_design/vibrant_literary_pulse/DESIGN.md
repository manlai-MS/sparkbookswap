## Brand & Style

This design system captures an energetic, culturally fresh literary exchange aesthetic—merging the intellectual delight of book sharing with youthful, forward-leaning momentum. The emotional goal is immediate creative invigoration: curiosity, community kinship, and tactile modern sophistication. 

The aesthetic style merges **High-Contrast Modernism** with polished **Glassmorphism accents**. Clean, luminous canvases offset electric bursts of chromatic energy, establishing an interface that feels like an avant-garde editorial platform fused with an active social network. It balances structural clarity with unexpected vibrancy, eschewing passive library tones in favor of bold, inspiring visual dialogues.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-voltage tension between marine teal and deep berry purple, anchored against ultra-crisp whites and deep ink neutrals:

- **Primary (`#0d9488` / Vibrant Teal):** Used for primary conversion points, active indicators, and high-energy interactive components. Represents intellectual freshness and clarity.
- **Secondary (`#9333ea` / Rich Berry Plum):** Used for expressive badges, featured curators, exclusive book tags, and celebratory moments. Introduces warmth, creative depth, and artistic prestige.
- **Tertiary (`#14b8a6` / Bright Cyan-Green):** Deployed for subtle state transitions, hover effects, positive swap confirmations, and accent highlights.
- **Neutral Base & Canvas:**
  - Page Background: `#f8fafc` (Ultra-clean slate tint).
  - Elevated Container / Cards: `#ffffff` (Pure optic white).
  - Primary Text / Structural Ink: `#0f172a` (Slate ink).
  - Secondary Text / Metadata: `#64748b` (Muted cool gray).
  - Hairline Borders: `#e2e8f0` (Subtle boundary definition).

Reserve pure purple-to-teal linear gradients exclusively for hero milestones, community achievements, and interactive visual accents.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the electric, geometric personality of **Space Grotesk** for display, headlines, and data-labels with the high-legibility, open-aperture humanist balance of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for extended prose, summaries, and reading notes.

- **Headlines & Expressive Titles:** Space Grotesk provides a technical yet friendly modern vibe. Tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) on larger headers produces a punchy editorial posture.
- **Reading Body & Longcopy:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures fatigue-free readability across descriptions, literary excerpts, and user reviews.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Space Grotesk in uppercase or semi-bold weights handles categories, genre chips, and numeric counters with geometric precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an adaptable **12-column fluid grid** for desktop, collapsing to **8 columns** on tablet and **4 columns** on mobile devices.

- **Grid Geometry:** 
  - Desktop: 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, maximum content container width of `1280px` centered within a `3rem` canvas margin.
  - Mobile: 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `1.25rem` (`20px`) outer margin.
- **Rhythm & Stacking:** Micro-spacing relies on an 8-point base scale (`0.25rem` up to `2.5rem`). Group related metadata closely using `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while separating book showcase showcases and profile clusters using `space-xl` to maintain an airy, gallery-like feel.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered chromatic luminosity and ultra-diffused shadows, preventing flat layouts without resorting to heavy skeuomorphism.

- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#f8fafc` flat background.
- **Surface Level 1 (Default Cards & Shelves):** Solid `#ffffff` framed by an ultra-subtle border (`1px solid #f1f5f9`) and a soft ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active Swaps):** `#ffffff` with a teal-tinted ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Nav):** Translucent glassmorphism using `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, coupled with a dual plum-tinted drop shadow: `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(147, 51, 234, 0.14), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a welcoming, tactile curvature (`roundedness: 2`), projecting an inviting, community-first feel without sacrificing contemporary sleekness.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Chips):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) to `0.75rem` (`12px`) border-radius.
- **Cards & Content Blocks:** `1rem` (`16px`) border-radius (`rounded-lg`), delivering soft, friendly book containers.
- **Hero Containers & Feature Modules:** `1.5rem` (`24px`) border-radius (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pills / Status Chips:** Full circular radii (`9999px`) for genre badges, availability tags, and active exchange tokens.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Swap / Request):** Background `#0d9488`, text `#ffffff`, font `Space Grotesk` medium/semi-bold. Hover initiates a transition to `#0f766e` with a localized teal glow (`0 0 16px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.35)`). Active scales down to `0.98`.
- **Secondary Action (Curate / Bookmark):** Background `#f3e8ff`, text `#9333ea`, border `1px solid rgba(147, 51, 234, 0.2)`. On hover: background `#ede9fe`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Action:** Transparent background, text `#0f172a`, underline hover or subtle `#f1f5f9` fill.

### Chips & Badges
- **Genre & Status Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, Space Grotesk `label-md`. 
  - *Available for Swap:* Teal background (`#ccfbf1`), teal text (`#0f766e`).
  - *Community Favorite / Spark Pick:* Berry plum background (`#f3e8ff`), plum text (`#7e22ce`).

### Book Cards & Showcase Units
- **Structure:** Clean white surface (`#ffffff`), `1rem` rounded corners, `1px` border in `#f1f5f9`. 
- **Media Presentation:** Book covers sit with an aspect ratio of `2:3`, framed with a minimal `0.5rem` inset padding and a delicate inner shadow simulating page thickness.
- **Interactive State:** Hover elevates card to Level 2 elevation with a subtle `-4px` vertical translate.

### Form Inputs
- Field backgrounds set to `#ffffff` with a `1.5px` border in `#e2e8f0`. Focus states transition seamlessly to a vibrant teal border (`#0d9488`) backed by an outer ring glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`. Floating labels utilize Space Grotesk `label-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with `4px` radius for checkboxes; fully rounded for radios. Inactive border is `#cbd5e1`. Checked state transitions to a solid `#9333ea` fill housing a crisp white geometric icon checkmark.

### Bespoke System Component: Swap Exchange Tracker
- A linear flow component linking the book owner to the recipient. Illustrated as a segmented progress ribbon using a dual gradient (`#0d9488` transitioning into `#9333ea`) with frosted pill milestones indicating "Handshake Initiated," "In Transit," and "Completed Read."
